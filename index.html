Privacy Policy — OETARIS

Last updated: 7 November 2026

OETARIS is a study tool for the OET Medicine sub-tests, published by Bana Apps. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version is that it does not collect any.

Data we collect

None. OETARIS does not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDK of any kind.

Network access

The app requests no internet permission and makes no network requests. All of its content is contained within the app itself and it works entirely offline.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your study progress locally so that it is still there when you reopen it. This includes:

  • Which study-plan tasks you have ticked off
  • Your chosen test date
  • Scores you record for writing, speaking, reading and mock attempts
  • Any letter text you type into the writing practice screen
  • Your light or dark theme preference

This information is held in your device's local browser storage, inside the app's own private storage area. It never leaves your device and is never visible to us or to anyone else.

Deleting your information

Open the Progress tab and use Erase to delete everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is no server-side copy to request the deletion of.

The Progress tab also has an Export option, which shows your saved data as text so you can copy it to another device. What you do with that text is entirely under your control.

Backups

If you have Android backup enabled, your device may include the app's stored progress in your own Google account backup. That backup is governed by Google's privacy policy and your device settings, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can disable it in your device's backup settings.

Permissions

OETARIS declares no Android permissions. It has no access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, phone state or any other device feature.

Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals preparing for a professional English examination. It is not directed at children, and because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.

Not affiliated with OET

OETARIS is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to OET or Cambridge Boxhill Language Assessment. All practice material in the app is original and written for language practice only.
